# Participant Task Sheet

ResearchForge helps people inspect an AI-assisted company-research result. Please work on your own
and say what you expect as you go. There is no penalty for getting stuck; confusion is useful
product feedback.

## Scenario

You want to understand whether 宁德时代's reported profit in `2024H1` was supported by operating
cash flow.

## Tasks

1. Select 宁德时代 and `2024H1`.
2. Ask: `2024 年上半年利润是否真正转化成了经营现金流？`
3. Start the research and explain the conclusion in your own words.
4. Find one important financial fact used in the conclusion.
5. Find how one numeric result was calculated.
6. Open one supporting evidence item and identify its page in the official filing.
7. Find one counter-evidence item or limitation that reduces certainty.
8. Explain one thing the monitoring plan says to check in the next comparable filing.

When finished, describe one part that worked well, one confusing part and one change you would
most want. Do not enter personal financial information.
