# ResearchForge Usability Pilot Privacy Notice

Version: `v1.0`
Evidence type: `REAL_HUMAN` only when a real participant gives consent

## Purpose

This 15–20 minute session tests whether the ResearchForge interface makes a company-research
result understandable and independently checkable. It does not test the participant's financial
knowledge and does not provide investment advice.

## What is recorded

- a pseudonym such as `participant_001`;
- broad target-user group and prior research experience;
- whether each task was completed without directional help;
- short notes about confusion, useful elements and requested changes;
- session and product-run identifiers.

Names, email addresses, employer names, account credentials, investment holdings and financial
decisions are not requested. Screen or audio recording is optional and requires separate consent.

## Choice and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. The participant may stop, skip a question or withdraw the record at
any time. A withdrawn session is marked `withdrawn` and is not counted. Declining a recording does
not prevent participation.

## Storage and reporting

Raw session records are kept in ignored local project storage. Only aggregated outcomes and
privacy-minimized feedback may be published after review. Simulated AI sessions are labeled
`SIMULATED` and never counted as people.

Consent prompt: “I understand the purpose, the recorded fields and my right to stop or withdraw.
I agree to participate.” Record only the yes/no result, never a signature in Git.
