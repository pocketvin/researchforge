# ResearchForge V1.4 → V1.5 Productization Change Note

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: **ACCEPTED PRODUCT-DIRECTION CHANGE**

## Why the version changed

V1.4 proved a substantial engineering baseline and completed two controlled Skill Evolution
experiments. The experiments ended honestly at
`RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS_UNSUPPORTED_AFTER_TWO_EXPERIMENTS`. Making a supported Evolution hypothesis
the definition of overall project success no longer serves the most useful product or career
story.

V1.5 therefore changes the primary product thesis from “financial research plus a controlled
learning experiment” to an evidence-grounded A-share fundamental-research workspace.

## New product success definition

A real user can select a real company and period, ask a real research question, and receive a
result based on real public disclosures whose conclusion, facts, formulas, evidence, counter
evidence, limitations and monitoring items can be independently inspected.

## What changes

- Product narrative, status, portfolio and demo lead with the Research workspace.
- Real public-disclosure ingestion becomes P0.
- Product, fixture and benchmark data become explicit isolated namespaces.
- Research UX prioritizes Company, Period, Question and progressive disclosure.
- Skill Lab becomes the secondary, experimental, read-only Quality Lab.
- Real human pilot readiness replaces simulated personas as the next usefulness milestone.
- V1.5 success labels do not depend on Evolution hypothesis support.

## What remains unchanged

- V1.2, V1.3 and V1.4 historical scope/schema packages.
- The V1.4 finance, evidence, verifier and ten-stage LangGraph ownership boundaries.
- Primary and contingency benchmark packages, thresholds, hashes and denominators.
- Both formal experiment results and the two-experiment stopping rule.
- The rule that `SIMULATED` evidence is not human evidence.
- Non-goals: trading, prediction, multi-agent systems, complex RAG, open-ended self-modification,
  full-market coverage and enterprise infrastructure.

## Version-name clarification

The existing identifier `experiment_contingency_v1_5_001` referred to the once-only contingency
experiment defined by the V1.4 research protocol. It remains immutable historical evidence. It
does not govern, and must not be confused with, the V1.5 Productization scope.

## Schema impact

V1.4 schemas are not mutated. New product-data namespace, ingestion or artifact semantics require
new V1.5 schemas and migration rules. Unchanged V1.4 artifacts remain readable and verifiable.

## Authority

[`researchforge-v1.5-product-thesis.md`](researchforge-v1.5-product-thesis.md) is the highest
active product-direction authority. Preserved V1.4 contracts continue to govern unchanged
financial, evidence, workflow and safety semantics until a V1.5 contract explicitly supersedes
them.
