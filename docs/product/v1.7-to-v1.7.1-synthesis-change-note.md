# ResearchForge V1.7 → V1.7.1 Research Synthesis Change Note

**Date:** 2026-09-05
**Status:** Engineering verified; owner re-acceptance pending

## Why this patch exists

V1.7 solved autonomous filing discovery, broad question routing and full-filing Evidence retrieval, but the first owner test exposed a product-quality failure: the running demo had inherited deterministic mode and its General Research fallback promoted filing excerpts into Findings and Deep Analysis. The result was auditable, but it was not real research synthesis.

The same test also exposed a Router precedence bug: a comprehensive question containing “业务结构” could be routed to the narrower Business Analysis skill instead of Company Overview.

V1.7 Owner Acceptance therefore remains failed. V1.7.1 is the corrective iteration before Release Freeze.

## Product contract

General Research now exposes `synthesis_mode`:

- `model`: evidence-constrained AI Research Synthesis was executed;
- `evidence_summary_fallback`: only verified Evidence/facts are summarized and the UI explicitly says AI synthesis was not executed.

The fallback is no longer allowed to look like a full analyst report.

## Synthesis rules

A model-backed General Research result must:

- answer the user’s question directly in the Executive Summary;
- synthesize across retrieved chunks instead of copying long filing excerpts;
- use analytical Finding titles and explain why each finding matters;
- attach only Evidence IDs supplied by the current retrieval/counter-retrieval path;
- attach Fact IDs only when the Finding actually discusses that metric;
- distinguish verified observations, supported inference, causal hypotheses, risks and outlook honestly;
- use analytical Deep Analysis headings rather than raw filing section names;
- keep raw PDF/table/check-box noise out of the primary report;
- provide a substantive Overall Judgment rationale.

For `company_overview`, evidence permitting, the model must cover at least five findings and five analytical dimensions, including performance/growth, profitability/cash quality, business mix and risks/outlook.

## Runtime isolation

`scripts/start_demo.py` now defaults to and actively writes `RESEARCHFORGE_REASONING_MODE=auto`. An inherited CI/test environment can no longer silently leave the owner demo in deterministic mode. Deterministic remains an explicit option for reproducible zero-cost tests and packaging smoke.

## Report UX

The Web surface is synthesis-first and evidence-on-demand:

- a visible `MODEL SYNTHESIS` or `EVIDENCE SUMMARY` badge identifies the mode;
- fallback runs show a prominent warning that AI synthesis was not executed;
- each Finding renders as a concise analytical headline plus explanation;
- irrelevant Fact chips are removed at backend assembly time;
- Claim-level supporting Evidence is collapsed by default and can be expanded for audit;
- the full Supporting Evidence and Trace remain available separately.

## Real model verification

The patched model contract has been exercised against official-source full-text packages in all three supported markets:

- 贵州茅台 comprehensive analysis → `company_overview`, 8 Claims, 5 analytical sections;
- NVIDIA growth analysis → `growth_analysis`, 6 Claims, 5 analytical sections;
- 腾讯 business-structure analysis → `business_analysis`, 6 Claims, 5 analytical sections.

All three runs reported `synthesis_mode=model`; their primary report text contained none of the old “官方披露在该部分记录” template or `√适用 / □不适用` PDF noise.

The full engineering gate passed on V1.7.1. Owner re-acceptance remains the only Release Freeze action.
